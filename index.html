Daily tips

One tip a day. Save it, try it, play better.

Short, practical fixes for everyday golfers, the same ones we post on Instagram.

Grip

Good golf starts with your hands

Light pressure. Fingers, not palm. Let the club do the work.

Tempo

Smooth beats hard. Every time.

Swing at 80% and watch the ball go further.

Irons

Your divot tells the truth

Ball first, then turf. If your divot starts behind the ball, shift your weight to your lead side at impact.

Putting

Long putts are about pace, not line

Picture a 3-foot circle around the hole and just get it in there. Fewer 3-putts, instantly.

Short game

Hit the sand, not the ball

Open the face, swing through, let it splash out.

Practice

10 balls with a plan beat a bucket of 100

One target. One goal. Full pre-shot routine on every ball.
