Hand-scratched

No printing, no laser. Every hair is a single cut made with a blade or needle.

Real depth

Fur, face and nose sit on separate sheets, so the portrait has true depth when the light moves.

Made to keep

Lines are cut, not painted, so they never fade. Framed in oak behind a clear front sheet.

    Questions

    Anything else? Ask on WhatsApp
    What photos work best?

    A few clear photos at their eye level, in soft daylight, with the eyes in focus. Phone photos are fine. More angles help us get the details right.

    Can you do two pets in one portrait?

    Yes. Each additional pet in the same portrait adds 40% to the price.

    Can you make a memorial portrait?

    Yes, and we are glad to. Older or blurry photos are welcome. Send everything you have and we will work from all of them.

    How long does it take?

    4 – 6 weeks depending on size, from your photos to delivery. If you need it by a certain date, tell us before you order.

    Do you ship to my country?

    We ship worldwide, insured and packed in a wooden crate. Shipping is quoted with your order.

    How do I look after it?

    Dust it with a soft dry cloth and keep it out of strong direct sunlight. The lines are cut into the acrylic, so they will not fade.
