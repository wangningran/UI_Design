## Direction agreed so far
- Cool grey and black, one sans-serif typeface (Inter Tight), lots of whitespace, English copy.
- Real photos of real work only, no AI-generated images.
- Signature effect: the acrylic sheets of a portrait stacking and separating.
- Brand voice and social avatar lean cute and warm (it is a pet business), drawn in the white "scratched fur" style.
